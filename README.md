# Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader

A single fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been
liquidated, read at 3 a.m. with an empty wallet and a stubborn sense of
humour.

> You will be rich again soon, says the cookie, which has never once bothered to check your empty balance.

The cookie opens with a confident promise — you will be rich again soon —
then undercuts it in the same breath: the cookie has never once bothered
to check your empty balance. The joke lands in that gap between the bold
prediction and the unchecked wallet behind it. Warm, reckless, and gone
in one breath.

`main.ts` reads `entry.txt` at runtime and writes it unchanged to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, refusing empty, over-long, or over-word
entries.
